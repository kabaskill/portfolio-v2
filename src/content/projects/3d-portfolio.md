The 3D portfolio gallery turns the work archive into a navigable scene. Each project becomes a panel in a WebGL space, so browsing involves moving through the room, focusing on a frame, and choosing whether to follow its link. The spatial layout also gives sound and music projects a different kind of presence than a standard grid can provide.

I built the scene with Three.js and React Three Fiber and handled the interaction design, project loading, navigation, and visual system. The gallery is an experiment in whether a portfolio can feel like a place without making the work harder to reach.
