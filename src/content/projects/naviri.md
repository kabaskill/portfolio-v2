Naviri is a startup operating system for founders who want a clearer path to product-market fit. I worked with the team in 2025 as a full-stack developer, building both the public website and the AI-powered application behind it.

The product brings several parts of early-stage company work into one place. Unify turns scattered project information into a shared source of truth. Detect looks for risks, weak claims, and missing context before they become expensive assumptions. Plan turns the next steps into a custom roadmap. Consult gives founders a way to ask questions about their business and receive focused analysis.

I worked across the marketing site and the application itself. The stack included Remix, TypeScript, Firebase, and Tailwind CSS. The job was not just to make a polished front end. It was to connect the product's data, user flows, and AI features into something founders could actually use while shaping a company.
