Blof was a rock-band from Turkey started late 2010s. I joined the band as a lead guitarist around 2017 and produced 4 singles together as well as numerous live performances, including a national TV appearance on TV8's Rising Star.

From the technical perspective, I was a core part of the whole production of said 4 singles regarding; recording, mixing and arrangement.
