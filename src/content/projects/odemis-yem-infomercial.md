I handled sound design, voice-over editing, and the soundtrack for this Odemis Yem infomercial. The video was made for a local agricultural company and moves between farm footage, product shots, and direct information about the brand. The mix had to keep the spoken content intelligible while giving the images enough transition and production detail to move cleanly from one section to the next.
