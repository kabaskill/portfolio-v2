A gamified music-theory application that turns listening, notation, and repetition into an approachable learning loop.

Fugue State is a browser-based music theory game built around chords, listening, and repetition. It uses a deck-builder structure to turn practice into play, then asks the player to recognize the same musical idea through scientific notation, do-re-mi syllables, and chromatic names. That change of viewpoint is the point. The player has to listen and read, not just memorize one label.

The project uses React and Vite, with Tonal.js for music theory, Tone.js for synthesis, abc.js for notation, and drag-and-drop tools for the cards. I also used Preact Signals, Framer Motion, and Tailwind CSS. I worked on the product idea, interface, game rules, and implementation. The repository and live version are linked with the project.
