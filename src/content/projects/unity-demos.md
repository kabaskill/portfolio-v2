This pair of Unity demos contains two small game studies. Bedroom Guitarist tests a clicker loop around practising guitar. The Angry Birds clone tests aiming, trajectory, collision, and rigid-body behaviour after a launch.

I implemented both projects in Unity. They are small studies rather than finished commercial games; the browser builds contain the playable versions.
