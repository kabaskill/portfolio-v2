Unides and Yawara are two sides of the same Berlin-based creative and events project. They were founded by the same person, but they answer different needs. Unides is the talent and representation platform. Yawara is the public agenda for Latin American events across Europe. I worked as the full-stack developer for both.

Unides connects models, actors, dancers, directors, content creators, photographers, stylists, makeup artists, art directors, tattoo artists, and other creative workers with brands, festivals, stages, and screens. The site has talent profiles, sedcard-style presentation, applications, team and agency information, and a clear route for clients and artists to get in touch. Its larger purpose is to support Latinx talent and culture in Europe without sanding away the identity of the people it represents.

Yawara turns that same community focus toward events. It lets people search and filter an agenda of reggaeton and cumbia nights, samba and forro parties, festivals, and community gatherings across Europe. The platform includes event pages, organizer-facing information, interest actions, and newsletter signup. Building the two together meant working across public content, profiles, event data, search, and the practical details of an active event business. The two live sites are linked separately because they share an origin but serve different audiences.
