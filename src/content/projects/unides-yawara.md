Unides and Yawara are two related web applications. Unides is the talent and representation platform, while Yawara is an events agenda for Latin American events across Europe. I worked as the full-stack developer for both, building talent profiles, sedcard-style pages, applications, agency information, and contact flows for artists and clients on Unides.

For Yawara, I built event pages, category and location filters, organizer information, interest actions, and newsletter signup. The live sites show both applications in their current public context.
