Benzersiz follows a pharmacist whose life changes after a violent injury. The film moves from an ordinary pharmacy environment into a revenge thriller. İlker Sarı directed the film and also contributed to its sound effects.

I handled sound design and final mix for the movie. The sound team included Semih Turgut, Okan Selçuk, Ahmet Şengün, and Mehmet Yerebakan, with additional effects by Hüseyin Eter Baş.
